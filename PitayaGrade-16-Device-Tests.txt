PITAYAGRADE — 16 ANDROID DEVICE TESTS
=====================================

1. Clean installation and offline first run
   Install and open PitayaGrade without internet. Confirm that the app opens
   correctly and honestly reports which models are available.

2. Camera permission denied
   Open the live camera, deny camera access, and confirm that the app displays
   a clear message and remains usable.

3. Camera permission granted
   Allow camera access and confirm that the camera preview opens and can be
   stopped without leaving the camera active.

4. Gallery scan — clear dragon fruit
   Select a clear dragon-fruit photo and analyze it. Confirm that the app shows
   a reviewable result or an honest rejection with the model identity.

5. Gallery scan — non-fruit image
   Select a face or an unrelated object. Confirm that it is rejected instead
   of being confidently graded as dragon fruit.

6. Live-camera dragon-fruit scan
   Scan one real dragon fruit using the live camera. Confirm that the scan
   completes and produces only one reviewable result.

7. Model selection and persistence
   Switch between every available verified model. Close and reopen the app,
   then confirm that the last valid model selection remains active.

8. Save a scan record
   Save one scan result. Confirm that it appears only once in History and
   includes its model and scanning method.

9. History search and notes
   Search for a saved record, add a note, leave History, and return. Confirm
   that the correct record is found and the note remains saved.

10. Analytics and date-bounded report
    Generate a report for a selected date range. Confirm that its totals match
    the saved records within that range.

11. Notification preferences and read status
    Change the scan-notification setting, produce an alert, mark it as read,
    and reopen Notifications. Confirm that both settings remain saved.

12. CSV export, cancellation, and retry
    Start an export, cancel the save operation, and then retry. Confirm that
    cancellation causes no error and the retry creates one readable CSV file.

13. Background and resume
    Leave the app running in the background for 30 seconds and reopen it.
    Confirm that it resumes without freezing, duplicating results, or losing
    saved information.

14. Rotation and interrupted scan
    Start a scan, rotate the phone, leave the scan page, and return. Confirm
    that the interface remains usable and no camera stream is left running.

15. Repeated Analyze-button taps
    Tap Analyze repeatedly in quick succession. Confirm that only one analysis
    and one saved result are created.

16. Force-stop and device restart
    Force-stop the app and reopen it. Restart the phone and reopen the app
    again. Confirm that saved records and valid settings remain available.


WHAT TO RECORD FOR EVERY TEST
=============================

- Test date and time
- Phone model
- Android version
- What actually happened
- Status: Pass, Fail, or Blocked
- Screenshot or video filename
- Defect ID if a problem was discovered (for example, BUG-01)
- Anonymous tester ID (for example, tester-01)

You do not need 16 different dragon fruits. A few clear fruit photos, one
non-fruit photo, and one real dragon fruit for the live-camera test are enough.
