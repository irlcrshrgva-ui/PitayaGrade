Common questions

Before you report a problem.

Why does Android warn me about the APK?

The current file is a testing build installed outside an app store. Verify that it came from this website, then follow Android’s permission prompt only if you intend to participate in testing.

Does the website upload my fruit photos?

No. The public website provides information and downloads. Current scan records and selected images are handled locally by the application.

Are visible-symptom results a confirmed diagnosis?

No. They are AI-assisted visible-symptom estimates and must not replace qualified agricultural or laboratory assessment.
