More than navigation

What the 16 device tests actually check.

Navigation is only a small part. The worksheet checks whether the installed Android app behaves correctly under real conditions and preserves trustworthy results.

  1. Setup
    Clean install and offline first run

    Install while offline and confirm the app opens with an honest model status.

  2. Permission
    Camera permission denied

    Deny access and confirm the app explains the problem without freezing.

  3. Permission
    Camera permission granted

    Grant access and confirm the preview opens and stops correctly.

  4. Scanning
    Gallery scan with a clear dragon fruit

    Upload a clear fruit photo and inspect the model identity and result.

  5. Scanning
    Gallery scan with a non-fruit image

    Use a face or unrelated object and confirm it is rejected instead of graded.

  6. Scanning
    Live-camera dragon-fruit scan

    Scan a real fruit and confirm only one reviewable result is produced.

  7. Model
    Model selector and persistence

    Switch among available verified models and confirm the choice persists.

  8. Records
    Save scan record

    Save one result and confirm its model and method are recorded once.

  9. Records
    History search and note

    Find a record, add a note, leave the page, and confirm the note remains.

  10. Reports
    Analytics and date-bounded report

    Confirm totals match the records inside the selected dates.

  11. Alerts
    Notification preference and read state

    Toggle alerts and confirm preferences and read status survive reopening.

  12. Export
    CSV export, cancellation, and retry

    Cancel once, retry, and confirm one readable CSV is saved.

  13. Stability
    Background and resume

    Leave the app for 30 seconds and confirm it resumes without duplicate work.

  14. Stability
    Rotation and interrupted scan

    Rotate and leave during scanning; confirm no camera stream is abandoned.

  15. Stability
    Repeated scan taps

    Tap Analyze repeatedly and confirm only one analysis is created.

  16. Stability
    Force-stop and device restart

    Restart the app and phone, then confirm saved records and settings remain.

For every test, record

What actually happened, Pass/Fail/Blocked, phone model, Android version, time, tester, and the screenshot or video filename.

You do not need 16 different fruits

A few clear fruit photos, one non-fruit photo, and one real fruit for the camera test are enough. The tests measure app behavior, not 16 separate model predictions.

Recommended sequence

One phone first, then the group.

Pilot the APK

One group member runs all device rows and records screenshots or screen recordings in a private evidence folder.

Resolve blockers

Report failures through Contact, retest affected rows after a new build, and never overwrite the original evidence.

Conduct approved UAT

Use anonymous IDs, observe without coaching, and preserve the genuine measurements and comments.
