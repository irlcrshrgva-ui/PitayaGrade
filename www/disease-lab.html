Disease label lab

Experimental image classification for dragon-fruit fruit and stems. This predicts the dataset’s labels; it does not measure disease area or change quality grades.

Load the evaluated experimental model to begin.

    Scores are uncalibrated. An unrelated image can receive a high score. A healthy label does not establish that a plant is disease-free. Seek an agricultural expert for diagnosis.

    Labels retain the publisher’s plant-part distinctions and broad categories such as “Fungi.” The first archive’s four Good/Bad labels are a separate task; they do not identify specific diseases. Image-level labels supply no lesion outlines.

    Sources under CC BY 4.0: Sarkar, Pranta and Mojumdar (2024) and Pranto, Dhar and Ayon (2026). Images are resized, deduplicated, mapped to source-preserving labels and partitioned for this experiment.

    Visual duplicate grouping reduces leakage. Plant and capture-session identifiers are unavailable, so the test set does not establish performance on new farms or plants.
